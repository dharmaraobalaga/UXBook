Q106. Accessibility vs Usability
Accessibility (అందుబాటులో ఉండేలా డిజైన్ చేయడం)

విభిన్న సామర్థ్యాలు ఉన్న వ్యక్తు లు కూడా digital products‌ను perceive, understand, navigate, and interact చేయగలిగేలా డిజైన్ చేయడం.

English: Accessibility ensures that digital products can be used by people with diverse abilities, including people with visual, auditory, motor, and
cognitive disabilities.

Usability (సులభంగా ఉపయోగించగలగడం)

ఒక ప్రొ డక్ట్‌ను యూజర్లు సులభంగా, సమర్థవంతంగా, సంతృప్తికరంగా ఉపయోగించగలిగేలా డిజైన్ చేయడం.

English: Usability measures how effectively, efficiently, and satisfactorily users can achieve their goals.

Accessibility                                             Usability

Inclusive access                                          Ease of use

Considers diverse abilities                               Considers user effectiveness

Screen reader support                                     Simple navigation

Keyboard accessibility                                    Efficient task completion

Accessible interaction                                    Understandable interaction


Practical Example – Banking App

Accessibility:

     Blind users కోసం screen reader labels.
     Motor disabilities ఉన్నవారికి keyboard navigation.
     Low vision users కోసం sufficient contrast.

Usability:

     Money transfer process‌ను simple steps‌లో రూపొందించడం.
     Clear instructions ఇవ్వడం.
     Unnecessary steps తగ్గించడం.

Interview Answer:

"Accessibility ensures that people with diverse abilities can access and interact with a product, while usability focuses on how easily and effectively users
can achieve their goals. Both are essential for creating inclusive digital experiences."




Q107. Accessibility vs Inclusive Design
Accessibility

ప్రత్యేకంగా disabilities ఉన్న వ్యక్తు లు digital products‌ను ఉపయోగించగలిగేలా barriers తొలగించడం.

English: Accessibility focuses on removing barriers that prevent people with disabilities from using products and services.

Inclusive Design
విభిన్న backgrounds, abilities, ages, languages, cultures, and circumstances ఉన్న వ్యక్తు ల అవసరాలను design process‌లో పరిగణనలోకి తీసుకోవడం.

English: Inclusive Design is an approach that considers human diversity and includes people with different needs and circumstances throughout the
design process.

Accessibility                                              Inclusive Design

Removes access barriers                                    Considers human diversity

Often focuses on disabilities                              Broader range of differences

Standards and guidelines                                   Research and design approach

WCAG is an important reference                             Co-design and diverse research


Practical Example – E-commerce App

Accessibility:

     Screen reader support.
     Keyboard navigation.
     Text contrast.

Inclusive Design:

     Multiple language options.
     Simple language for different literacy levels.
     Different payment methods.
     Support for different age groups and user needs.

Interview Answer:

"Accessibility focuses on ensuring that people with disabilities can use a product, while Inclusive Design is a broader approach that considers diverse
human needs, abilities, backgrounds, and situations throughout the design process."




Q108. Inclusive Design vs Universal Design
Inclusive Design

విభిన్న అవసరాలు ఉన్న వ్యక్తు లను design process‌లో భాగస్వామ్యం చేసి, వారి అవసరాలకు అనుగుణంగా solutions రూపొందించడం.

English: Inclusive Design involves designing with diverse people and adapting experiences to accommodate different needs.

Universal Design

సాధ్యమైనంత వరకు ఎక్కువ మంది వ్యక్తు లు ప్రత్యేక adaptations అవసరం లేకుండా ఉపయోగించగలిగే products and environments‌ను రూపొందించడం.

English: Universal Design aims to create products and environments usable by the widest range of people, to the greatest extent possible, without the
need for adaptation or specialized design.

Inclusive Design                                      Universal Design

Process-oriented approach                             Broad design philosophy

Includes diverse users in design                      Aims for broad usability

Adapts based on user needs                            Reduces need for special adaptations

Can support tailored solutions                        Seeks widely usable solutions


Practical Example – Public Website

Inclusive Design:

     Different language preferences తెలుసుకోవడానికి user research చేయడం.
     Users‌తో కలిసి design solutions test చేయడం.

Universal Design:

     Clear navigation.
     Readable text.
     Easy-to-use controls.
     Simple, consistent interactions.

Interview Answer:

"Inclusive Design emphasizes involving diverse users and addressing their specific needs, whereas Universal Design aims to create solutions that are
usable by the widest possible range of people without requiring special adaptations."




Q109. WCAG vs ADA
WCAG – Web Content Accessibility Guidelines

W3C యొక్క Web Accessibility Initiative (WAI) అభివృద్ధి చేసిన web accessibility guidelines.

English: WCAG provides internationally recognized technical guidelines for making web content more accessible to people with disabilities.

ADA – Americans with Disabilities Act

United States‌లో disabilities ఉన్న వ్యక్తు లపై discrimination‌ను నిషేధించే civil rights law.

English: ADA is a US civil rights law that prohibits discrimination against people with disabilities, including in areas covered by its requirements.

WCAG                                                            ADA

Technical guidelines                                            US civil rights law

Developed by W3C                                                US federal law

Explains accessibility requirements for web content             Prohibits disability discrimination

Uses conformance levels A, AA, AAA                              Legal obligations depend on applicable provisions

Internationally used                                            Applies within the United States




             WCAG – W3C Guidelines




             ADA – US Law



Important Interview Point:

WCAG అనేది law కాదు; అది technical accessibility guidelines. ADA అనేది US law. WCAG compliance అనేది అనేక సందర్భాల్లో web accessibilityని evaluate
చేయడానికి ఉపయోగించే ముఖ్యమైన technical reference.

Practical Example

ఒక US-based SaaS company తన website‌ను accessibility-friendlyగా రూపొందించాలనుకుంటే, WCAG guidelines‌ను technical implementation కోసం
ఉపయోగించవచ్చు. దాని legal obligations మాత్రం వర్తించే ADA provisions మరియు ఇతర చట్టా లపై ఆధారపడతాయి.

Interview Answer:

"WCAG is a set of technical accessibility guidelines developed by W3C, whereas ADA is a US civil rights law that prohibits disability discrimination.
WCAG helps teams implement accessible digital experiences, while ADA establishes legal obligations."

   Level AA

  ఎక్కువ web accessibility projects‌లో సాధారణంగా లక్ష్యంగా పెట్టు కునే level.
     Level AAA

    Highest conformance level
    మరింత కఠినమైన accessibility requirements. అన్ని content‌కు AAA సాధించడం ఎల్లప్పుడూ సాధ్యం కాకపోవచ్చు.

Practical Example

ఒక mobile banking app‌లో:
      Level A: అన్ని controls keyboard ద్వారా accessible‌గా ఉండాలి.
      Level AA: Normal text contrast కనీసం 4.5:1 ఉండాలి.

Q111. Alt Text vs ARIA Label
Alt Text (Alternative Text)

Image యొక్క meaning లేదా purpose‌ను text రూపంలో వివరించే alternative description.

English: Alt Text provides a textual alternative for meaningful images, especially for users relying on screen readers.

ARIA Label

Interactive element‌కు accessible name అందించడానికి ఉపయోగించే ARIA attribute.

English: aria-label provides an accessible name for an element when a suitable visible label is not available or when additional accessible naming
is needed.


    Alt Text Example


                     Product image
                     Alt: "Red mountain bicycle with black tires"




    ARIA Label Example

             Search button
             aria-label="Search products"



Alt Text                                                            ARIA Label

Describes image content or purpose                                  Names an interface element

Commonly uses alt attribute                                         Uses aria-label
Useful for meaningful images                          Useful for controls without visible labels

Can describe image function                           Can name buttons, controls, etc.


Important: Decorative images‌కు సాధారణంగా empty alt ( alt="" ) ఉపయోగిస్తా రు. Visible label ఉన్న button‌కు unnecessary aria-label పెట్టకుండా, visible
label‌ను accessible name‌గా ఉపయోగించడం మంచిది.

Interview Answer:

"Alt Text provides a textual alternative for meaningful images, while ARIA Label provides an accessible name for interface elements such as buttons and
controls. Both help assistive technologies communicate information to users."




Q112. Keyboard Navigation vs Screen Reader Navigation
Keyboard Navigation

Mouse ఉపయోగించకుండా keyboard ద్వారా website లేదా application‌ను navigate చేయడం.

Common keys:

     Tab – Next focusable element.
     Shift + Tab – Previous focusable element.
     Enter / Space – Activate controls.
     Arrow Keys – Navigate within certain controls.
     Esc – Close dialogs or menus, where supported.

English: Keyboard Navigation allows users to interact with interface elements using keyboard input instead of a mouse or touch.

Screen Reader Navigation

Screen reader software ద్వారా interface content‌ను speech లేదా braille రూపంలో తెలుసుకుని navigate చేయడం.

Examples: NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack.

English: Screen Reader Navigation enables users to explore content and understand interface semantics through assistive technology.

Keyboard Navigation                              Screen Reader Navigation

Keyboard-based interaction                       Assistive technology-based access

Focus order                                      Semantic reading and navigation

Tab, Enter, Space                                Headings, landmarks, labels

Supports motor accessibility                     Supports blind and low-vision users


Practical Example – Login Form

Keyboard user:

  1. Tab → Username.
  2. Tab → Password.
  3. Tab → Login button.
  4. Enter → Submit.

Screen reader user:

  1. Hears Username field label.
  2. Hears Password field label.
  3. Understands Login button purpose.
  4. Receives meaningful error messages.

Interview Answer:

"Keyboard Navigation enables users to operate an interface without a mouse, while Screen Reader Navigation helps users understand and navigate
content through assistive technology. A well-designed product should support both."




Q113. Color Blindness vs Low Vision
Color Blindness

కొన్ని colors మధ్య తేడాను గుర్తించడంలో ఇబ్బంది ఉండే visual condition.

English: Color blindness is a condition that affects how a person perceives certain colors, often making some color combinations difficult to distinguish.

Common types:

     Protanopia – Difficulty perceiving red.
     Deuteranopia – Difficulty perceiving green.
     Tritanopia – Difficulty perceiving blue-yellow differences.

Low Vision

Glasses లేదా ఇతర correction ఉపయోగించినా కూడా రోజువారీ పనుల్లో vision-related difficulty ఉండే పరిస్థితి .

English: Low vision refers to significant visual impairment that cannot be fully corrected with standard glasses, contact lenses, or treatment.




Color Blindness                                                                        Low Vision
Color differentiation challenges                                                       Difficulty seeing fine details


Color Blindness                                         Low Vision

Color perception differences                            Reduced visual function

Difficulty distinguishing colors                        Difficulty seeing details

Color combinations matter                               Text size and clarity matter

Use labels and patterns                                 Use zoom and readable layouts


Practical Example – Dashboard

Color Blindness:

     Red = Failed, Green = Success అని colors మాత్రమే వాడకూడదు.
     Labels, icons, patterns కూడా ఉపయోగించాలి.

Low Vision:

     Text resizing support.
     High contrast.
     Clear typography.
     Browser zoom support.

Interview Answer:

"Color blindness affects the ability to distinguish certain colors, while low vision involves reduced visual function. As designers, we should avoid color-
only communication and provide readable text, sufficient contrast, and flexible layouts."




Q114. Responsive Design vs Accessible Design
Responsive Design
వివిధ screen sizes మరియు devices‌కు అనుగుణంగా layout adapt అయ్యేలా డిజైన్ చేయడం.

English: Responsive Design ensures that a digital interface adapts to different screen sizes, orientations, and device contexts.

Accessible Design

విభిన్న abilities ఉన్న వ్యక్తు లు కూడా interface‌ను ఉపయోగించగలిగేలా డిజైన్ చేయడం.

English: Accessible Design ensures that interfaces can be perceived, understood, navigated, and operated by people with diverse abilities.

Responsive Design                                    Accessible Design

Adapts to screen sizes                               Adapts to diverse abilities

Mobile, tablet, desktop                              Visual, auditory, motor, cognitive needs

Flexible layouts                                     Assistive technology support

Breakpoints and fluid grids                          Semantic HTML and accessible controls


Practical Example – E-commerce Website

Responsive:

     Desktopలో 4 product columns.
     Tabletలో 2 columns.
     Mobileలో 1 column.

Accessible:

     Images‌కు meaningful alt text.
     Buttons‌కు accessible names.
     Keyboard navigation.
     Text contrast.

Interview Answer:

"Responsive Design adapts the interface to different devices and screen sizes, whereas Accessible Design ensures that people with diverse abilities can
use the interface. A successful digital product should be both responsive and accessible."




Q115. Accessibility Audit vs Usability Testing
Accessibility Audit

ఒక digital product accessibility guidelines మరియు standards‌ను ఎంతవరకు follow చేస్తుందో systematic‌గా evaluate చేయడం.

English: An Accessibility Audit evaluates a product against accessibility standards such as WCAG and identifies barriers affecting users with disabilities.

Usability Testing

నిజమైన లేదా representative users‌తో tasks చేయించి, product‌ను ఎంత సులభంగా ఉపయోగిస్తు న్నారో observe చేయడం.

English: Usability Testing involves observing users as they perform tasks to identify usability issues and improve the overall experience.

Accessibility Audit                                                    Usability Testing

Checks accessibility barriers                                          Checks usability problems

Uses WCAG criteria                                                     Uses task-based observation

Automated and manual checks                                            Moderated or unmoderated sessions

Expert evaluation and assistive technology testing                     Representative user participation

Focuses on accessibility conformance                                   Focuses on effectiveness and experience


Practical Example – Banking App

Accessibility Audit:

     Check contrast ratios.
     Verify form labels.
      Test keyboard access.
      Test with screen readers.
      Review focus indicators.

Usability Testing:

      Ask users to transfer money.
      Observe where they struggle.
      Measure task completion.
      Identify confusing instructions.

Interview Answer:

"An Accessibility Audit evaluates whether a product meets accessibility requirements and identifies barriers, while Usability Testing observes real users
performing tasks to understand how effectively they can use the product. Both methods complement each other."
