Q131. Qualitative Data vs Quantitative Data
Qualitative Data (గుణాత్మక సమాచారం)

యూజర్లు ఏం ఆలోచిస్తు న్నారు, ఎందుకు ఒక సమస్య ఎదుర్కొంటున్నారు, వారి భావాలు మరియు అనుభవాలు ఏమిటి అనే విషయాలను అర్థం చేసుకోవడానికి ఉపయోగించే
descriptive information.

English: Qualitative Data describes users' opinions, motivations, experiences, and behaviors, helping teams understand why something happens.

Examples:

     User interviews
     Open-ended survey responses
     Usability testing observations
     Customer feedback

Quantitative Data (పరిమాణాత్మక సమాచారం)

సంఖ్యల రూపంలో కొలవగలిగే information.

English: Quantitative Data consists of measurable numerical information used to identify patterns, compare performance, and measure outcomes.

Examples:

     Conversion rate
     Task completion rate
     Number of active users
     Time on task
     Revenue

Qualitative Data                                Quantitative Data

Descriptive                                     Numerical

Explains Why and How                            Explains How Much and How Often

Interviews                                      Analytics

User observations                               Conversion rates

Open-ended feedback                             Structured survey results


Practical Example – Classic Rummy

Qualitative:

     Users‌కు game table navigation confusing‌గా ఉందని feedback వచ్చింది.
     New users‌కు rules అర్థం కావడం లేదని usability testing‌లో గుర్తించారు.

Quantitative:

     35% users tutorial మధ్యలో exit అవుతున్నారు.
     Tutorial completion rate 65% ఉంది.
      Average time to start the first game 3 minutes.

Interview Answer:

"Qualitative data helps me understand why users behave in a particular way through interviews, observations, and feedback. Quantitative data helps me
measure how frequently or effectively they behave through metrics such as conversion rates, task completion, and engagement. I use both together to
make evidence-based design decisions."




Q132. KPI vs UX Metrics
KPI – Key Performance Indicator

ఒక business లేదా product యొక్క ముఖ్యమైన objectives సాధిస్తు న్నామా లేదా అనేది measure చేసే key metric.

English: A KPI is a measurable indicator used to evaluate progress toward important business or organizational objectives.

Examples:

      Revenue growth
      Customer acquisition
      Subscription conversion
      Customer retention

UX Metrics

ఒక product‌ను యూజర్లు ఎంత సులభంగా, సమర్థవంతంగా, సంతృప్తికరంగా ఉపయోగిస్తు న్నారో measure చేసే metrics.

English: UX Metrics measure the quality of user experiences, including usability, satisfaction, efficiency, and engagement.

KPI                                                       UX Metrics

Business performance indicator                            User experience measurement

Revenue and growth                                        Task success and usability

Business objectives                                       User-centered objectives

Conversion and retention                                  SUS, CSAT, task completion

Strategic measurement                                     Experience evaluation


Practical Example – E-commerce

Business KPI:

- Increase purchase conversion rate by 10%.

UX Metrics:

      Checkout task completion rate.
      Average time to complete checkout.
      Checkout error rate.
      Customer satisfaction.

Important: కొన్ని UX metrics కూడా business KPIs‌గా ఉపయోగించవచ్చు . ఇవి పూర్తిగా వేర్వేరు categories కావు.

Interview Answer:

"KPIs measure progress toward key business objectives, while UX metrics evaluate how effectively users interact with a product. I connect UX metrics
with business KPIs to understand how improvements in user experience contribute to product success."




Q133. DAU vs MAU
DAU – Daily Active Users

ఒక రోజులో product‌ను meaningful‌గా ఉపయోగించిన unique users సంఖ్య.

English: DAU measures the number of unique active users within a single day.

MAU – Monthly Active Users
ఒక నెలలో product‌ను meaningful‌గా ఉపయోగించిన unique users సంఖ్య.

English: MAU measures the number of unique active users within a month.

DAU                                                    MAU

Daily active users                                     Monthly active users

Daily engagement                                       Monthly engagement

Short-term usage                                       Longer-term usage

Daily product activity                                 Monthly product activity


Practical Example – Learning App

ఒక learning app‌లో:
      Monday active users = 5,000
      Tuesday active users = 5,500
      Monthly unique active users = 40,000

ఇక్కడ DAU అనేది ఒక్కో రోజు unique active users‌ను కొలుస్తుంది. MAU అనేది మొత్తం నెలలో కనీసం ఒకసారి active అయిన unique users‌ను కొలుస్తుంది.

  Illustrative User Activity

    DAU                                                                            MAU


    5,500                                                                          40,000
    Active users per day                                                           Unique users per month



Interview Answer:

"DAU measures the number of unique active users in a day, whereas MAU measures unique active users over a month. Together, they help evaluate
product engagement and usage patterns. The definition of an active user should be based on meaningful product activity."

Conversion Rate

ఒక specific desired action పూర్తి చేసిన users percentage.

Practical Example – E-commerce

    100 users purchase.

Q135. Bounce Rate vs Exit Rate
Bounce Rate

ఒక website‌లో user ఒక page‌ను visit చేసి, meaningful engagement లేదా further navigation లేకుండా session‌ను వదిలిపెట్టిన సందర్భాలను measure చేస్తుంది.

GA4లో Bounce Rate అనేది Engagement Rate‌కు complement. Engaged Session‌గా పరిగణించబడని sessions percentage.

English: In GA4, Bounce Rate is the percentage of sessions that were not engaged sessions.

Exit Rate

ఒక specific page‌ను చివరిగా చూసి users తమ session‌ను ముగించిన percentage.

English: Exit Rate measures the percentage of page views for a particular page that were the final page views of sessions.

Bounce Rate                                                      Exit Rate

Session-level concept                                            Page-level concept

GA4: Not engaged sessions                                        Page is last in session

Helps evaluate landing-page engagement                           Helps identify where users leave

Does not mean every user disliked the page                       Exit can be expected or normal


Practical Example – E-commerce Website

Page A – Product Landing Page:

    Users directly visit this page.
    They leave without engaging further.
    This may contribute to Bounce Rate.

Page B – Order Confirmation:

    Users complete purchase.
    They see confirmation page.
    They exit the website.

ఇక్కడ Order Confirmation page‌కు Exit Rate ఎక్కువగా ఉండవచ్చు. కానీ అది తప్పనిసరిగా UX సమస్య కాదు.

Interview Answer:

"Bounce Rate in GA4 measures the percentage of sessions that were not engaged, whereas Exit Rate measures how often a particular page is the last
page viewed in a session. I interpret both in context rather than assuming a high value always indicates a design problem."




Q136. Task Success Rate vs Task Completion Rate
ఈ రెండు terms చాలా సందర్భాల్లో similar meaning‌తో ఉపయోగిస్తా రు. అయితే usability evaluationలో వీటి మధ్య useful distinction ఇలా ఉంటుంది.

Task Success Rate

యూజర్లు ఒక task‌ను predefined success criteria ప్రకారం విజయవంతంగా పూర్తి చేసిన percentage.

English: Task Success Rate measures the percentage of users who successfully achieve a defined task goal.

Task Completion Rate

ఒక task‌ను పూర్తిగా finish చేసిన users percentage. కొన్ని testing frameworks‌లో ఇది Task Success Rate‌కు సమానంగా నిర్వచించబడుతుంది.

English: Task Completion Rate measures the percentage of users who complete a specified task, with the exact success criteria defined by the study.

Task Success Rate                                         Task Completion Rate

Focuses on achieving success criteria                     Focuses on task completion

May consider correct outcome                              Measures completed tasks

Partial or incorrect outcomes may fail                    Depends on defined completion rules

Usability outcome                                         Task completion outcome


Practical Example – Banking App

Task: Transfer ₹5,000 to another account.

     User A: Successfully transfers money.
     User B: Completes all steps but enters incorrect account details.
     User C: Leaves halfway.

Success rate‌ను predefined correct-transfer criteria ఆధారంగా measure చేస్తాం.

Task completion rate‌ను task పూర్తయిందా లేదా అనే study definition ఆధారంగా measure చేస్తాం.

Formula (when using binary completion):


                                                                                 Completed Tasks
                                                 Task Completion Rate =                          × 100
                                                                                  Total Attempts
                                                                                                      ​




Interview Answer:

"Task Success Rate focuses on whether users achieve the intended outcome correctly, while Task Completion Rate focuses on whether they finish the
task. In practice, the terms can overlap, so I always define success and completion criteria clearly before testing."




Q137. SUS vs NPS
SUS – System Usability Scale

ఒక product లేదా system usability‌ను measure చేయడానికి ఉపయోగించే standardized 10-question questionnaire.

English: SUS is a standardized questionnaire consisting of 10 statements that measures users' perceived usability of a system.

     10 questions.
     Five-point response scale.
     Score ranges from 0 to 100.
     Higher scores generally indicate better perceived usability.

NPS – Net Promoter Score

ఒక product లేదా service‌ను ఇతరులకు recommend చేసే user willingness‌ను measure చేసే metric.

English: NPS measures customer loyalty or advocacy by asking how likely users are to recommend a product or service to others.

Question:

"How likely are you to recommend this product to a friend or colleague?"

Response: 0–10 scale.
      Detractors: 0–6
      Passives: 7–8
      Promoters: 9–10


                                                           NP S = %P romoters − %Detractors


SUS                                                  NPS

Measures perceived usability                         Measures recommendation likelihood

10 questions                                         Usually one core question

Score 0–100                                          Score -100 to +100

Usability evaluation                                 Loyalty and advocacy

Product interaction experience                       Overall recommendation sentiment


Practical Example – Design Acharya Learning Platform

SUS:

      Course navigation easyగా ఉందా?
      Lessons access చేయడం simpleగా ఉందా?
      Students platform ఉపయోగించడంలో confident‌గా ఉన్నారా?

NPS:

- "Would you recommend Design Acharya to another student?"

Interview Answer:

"SUS is a standardized 10-item questionnaire used to assess perceived system usability, while NPS measures how likely customers are to recommend a
product. SUS focuses on usability, whereas NPS focuses on loyalty and advocacy."




Q138. CSAT vs CES
CSAT – Customer Satisfaction Score

ఒక product, service లేదా specific interaction పట్ల user satisfaction‌ను measure చేయడం.

English: CSAT measures how satisfied customers are with a product, service, or specific interaction.

Example question:

"How satisfied are you with your experience?"

CES – Customer Effort Score

ఒక task పూర్తి చేయడానికి user ఎంత effort పెట్టా ల్సి వచ్చిందో measure చేయడం.

English: CES measures how easy or difficult it was for customers to complete a particular task.

Example question:

"How easy was it to resolve your issue today?"

CSAT                                                               CES

Satisfaction                                                       Effort

How happy is the user?                                             How easy was the task?

Measures experience sentiment                                      Measures friction

Satisfaction surveys                                               Effort-based surveys


Practical Example – Customer Support Platform

CSAT:
- Support agent response పట్ల customer సంతృప్తిగా ఉన్నారా?

CES:

     Customer తన issue‌ను resolve చేయడానికి ఎన్ని steps తీసుకోవాల్సి వచ్చింది?
     Support ticket submit చేయడం easyగా ఉందా?

Interview Answer:

"CSAT measures how satisfied users are with an experience, while CES measures how much effort users need to complete a task. I use CSAT to
understand satisfaction and CES to identify unnecessary friction in user journeys."




Q139. A/B Testing vs Multivariate Testing
A/B Testing

ఒక design లేదా experience యొక్క రెండు versions‌ను వేర్వేరు user groups‌కు చూపించి, ఏ version better results ఇస్తుందో statistically evaluate చేయడం.

English: A/B Testing compares two versions of an experience to determine which performs better against a defined metric.

Example:

     Version A: Blue CTA button.
     Version B: Green CTA button.

Multivariate Testing (MVT)

ఒకే experiment‌లో multiple design elements యొక్క combinations‌ను పరీక్షించడం.

English: Multivariate Testing evaluates multiple combinations of changes across different elements to understand their individual and combined effects.

A/B Testing                                           Multivariate Testing

Compares versions                                     Compares combinations

Usually one main variation                            Multiple element variations

Simpler experiment                                    More complex experiment

Lower traffic needs in many cases                     Often requires more traffic

Identifies better variant                             Helps understand interactions between elements



  A/B Testing – Example

                                       Version A                                                                 Version B

                                         Buy Now                                                                  Buy Now


  Illustrative CTA color experiment, not real test results.


Practical Example – E-commerce Checkout

A/B Testing:

     A: "Continue to Payment"
     B: "Proceed to Pay"

Multivariate Testing:

     Headline: 2 versions.
     CTA color: 2 versions.
     CTA text: 2 versions.

Total possible combinations = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8.

Important: A/B testing మరియు multivariate testing రెండింటికీ సరైన experiment design, sufficient sample size, statistical analysis అవసరం.

Interview Answer:

"A/B Testing compares different versions of a design to identify which performs better, while Multivariate Testing evaluates multiple combinations of
design elements to understand their individual and combined impact. I select the method based on the hypothesis, traffic volume, and experiment
complexity."




Q140. Heatmap vs Session Recording
Heatmap

Users ఒక page‌లో ఎక్కడ click చేస్తు న్నారు, ఏ ప్రాంతాలపై ఎక్కువ attention చూపిస్తు న్నారు, ఎక్కడ scroll చేస్తు న్నారు అనే aggregate behavior‌ను visual representation
రూపంలో చూపిస్తుంది.

English: A Heatmap visualizes aggregated user interactions such as clicks, taps, scrolling, or attention patterns on a page.

Types:

     Click Heatmap
     Scroll Heatmap
     Move Heatmap

Session Recording

ఒక individual user session‌లో జరిగిన interactions‌ను playback రూపంలో చూడటానికి ఉపయోగించే tool.

English: Session Recording captures a replay of an individual user's interaction with a website or application, helping teams identify usability issues.

Heatmap                                                       Session Recording

Aggregated interaction patterns                               Individual session playback

Shows where users interact                                    Shows how users interact

Visual summary                                                Step-by-step behavior

Identifies interaction hotspots                               Reveals friction and confusion




Heatmap                                                                               Session Recording
Interaction patterns                                                                  Individual behavior

Practical Example – SaaS Dashboard

Heatmap:

     Users ఎక్కువగా ఏ navigation items click చేస్తు న్నారు?
     Primary CTA కనిపిస్తు న్నదా?
     Users page‌లో ఎంతవరకు scroll చేస్తు న్నారు?

Session Recording:

     Users filter apply చేయడంలో struggle అవుతున్నారా?
     Form errors తర్వాత ఏమి చేస్తు న్నారు?
     ఒక particular workflowలో ఎక్కడ ఆగిపోతున్నారు?

Interview Answer:
"Heatmaps show aggregated patterns of user interactions, while Session Recordings help us observe individual user journeys in detail. I use heatmaps
to identify patterns and recordings to investigate specific usability issues."

Privacy note: Session recording tools ఉపయోగించేటప్పుడు sensitive information masking, consent, and applicable privacy requirements తప్పనిసరిగా
పరిగణించాలి.



Q141. Funnel Analysis vs Cohort Analysis
Funnel Analysis

ఒక user goal పూర్తి చేయడానికి ఎన్ని steps దాటాలి, ఏ step వద్ద ఎక్కువ మంది drop-off అవుతున్నారు అనే విషయాలను analyze చేయడం.

English: Funnel Analysis measures how users progress through a sequence of steps toward a specific conversion goal and identifies drop-off points.

Cohort Analysis

ఒక common characteristic లేదా starting event ఆధారంగా users‌ను groups‌గా విభజించి, కాలక్రమంలో వారి behavior‌ను compare చేయడం.

English: Cohort Analysis groups users based on shared characteristics or experiences and tracks their behavior over time.

Funnel Analysis                                       Cohort Analysis

Step-by-step conversion                               Group-based behavior over time

Identifies drop-off                                   Identifies retention patterns

Signup and checkout                                   Monthly user groups

Conversion optimization                               Retention and engagement analysis



  Funnel Analysis – Signup Example
  Visit Website                                                                                                                                    1000


  Start Signup                                                                                                                                      700


  Verify Email                                                                                                                                      500


  Complete Signup                                                                                                                                   400



  Illustrative funnel: 40% of initial visitors completed signup.


Practical Example – Learning App

Funnel Analysis:

  1. Visit website.
  2. Register.
  3. Select course.
  4. Make payment.
  5. Start learning.

Cohort Analysis:

     January signup users.
     February signup users.
     March signup users.

ఈ cohorts‌లో 7-day retention, 30-day retention compare చేయవచ్చు.

Interview Answer:

"Funnel Analysis helps identify where users drop off during a sequence of actions, while Cohort Analysis tracks the behavior of specific user groups over
time. I use funnel analysis to improve conversion and cohort analysis to understand retention and long-term engagement."




Q142. Vanity Metrics vs Actionable Metrics
Vanity Metrics
చూడటానికి impressive‌గా కనిపించే కానీ business లేదా product decisions తీసుకోవడానికి ఉపయోగకరమైన insight ఇవ్వని metrics.

English: Vanity Metrics are numbers that may look impressive but do not necessarily provide meaningful insights into product performance or business
outcomes.

Examples:

      Total downloads without active usage.
      Total page views without conversion context.
      Total social media followers without engagement.

Actionable Metrics

ఒక meaningful decision లేదా product improvement‌కు దారితీసే metrics.

English: Actionable Metrics provide meaningful insights that help teams make informed decisions and improve product performance.

Examples:

      Checkout abandonment rate.
      Task success rate.
      30-day retention.
      Conversion rate.
      Support ticket resolution time.

Vanity Metrics                                Actionable Metrics

Looks impressive                              Drives decisions

Often surface-level                           Provides meaningful insights

Total downloads                               Active users

Total page views                              Qualified conversion

Total followers                               Engagement and retention


Practical Example – Design Acharya

Vanity Metrics:

      10,000 website visitors.
      5,000 social media followers.
      2,000 app downloads.

Actionable Metrics:

      500 qualified course enquiries.
      100 paid admissions.
      80% course completion.
      70% student retention.
      Placement and portfolio completion rates.

Interview Answer:

"Vanity Metrics show numbers that may look impressive but do not necessarily guide decisions. Actionable Metrics help teams understand actual user
behavior and business performance, enabling measurable product improvements."

Final advice: Move from Definitions to Decision-making
Senior-level interviews‌లో interviewer మీకు UX terminology తెలుసా అని మాత్రమే అడగరు. ఆ concepts‌ను real-world product problems‌లో ఎలా apply చేశారో
తెలుసుకోవాలనుకుంటారు.

ప్రతి question‌కు ఈ 4 steps‌లో సమాధానం సిద్ధం చేసుకోండి:
    1. Definition: Concept అంటే ఏమిటి?
    2. Difference: Related concept‌తో తేడా ఏమిటి?
    3. Application: Real project‌లో ఎలా ఉపయోగించారు?
    4. Impact: User లేదా business‌కు వచ్చిన measurable benefit ఏమిటి?

Example interview question:

"How do you measure the success of your design?"

Strong answer structure:

"I measure design success through a combination of usability metrics, user satisfaction, and business outcomes. For example, I track task success rate,
task completion time, conversion rate, and retention. I also combine quantitative analytics with qualitative user feedback to identify improvement
opportunities. My goal is to ensure that the design solves real user problems while contributing to business objectives."
